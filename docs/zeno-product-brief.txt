==================================================
0. THE PRODUCT
==================================================

Zeno is a school operating system designed first for schools in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

It is NOT merely:

- a student database
- an ERP
- a dashboard
- an attendance application
- a payment application

It is the operational system through which a school manages its daily life.

Zeno should connect:

School
→ Academic structure
→ Academic year
→ Classes
→ Students
→ Teachers
→ Staff
→ Subjects
→ Timetable
→ Attendance
→ Grades
→ Assessments
→ Reports
→ Documents
→ Administration
→ Finance
→ School history

The fundamental product philosophy is:

"COMPLEX BEHIND. SIMPLE IN FRONT."

The database, permissions, workflows and business logic may be complex.

The interface must feel simple.

A school director should not need months of training.

A teacher should be able to understand their dashboard immediately.

A secretary should be able to register a student quickly.

An accountant should immediately understand the financial situation.

==================================================
2. RESEARCH CONTEXT: DRC
==================================================

Zeno is designed for the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Do NOT assume a generic American, European or international school structure.

The official DRC education system includes:

- preschool / maternelle
- basic education
- secondary education / humanités
- general humanities
- technical humanities
- professional humanities
- technical and vocational education/training contexts

The primary cycle covers:

1ère primaire
2ème primaire
3ème primaire
4ème primaire
5ème primaire
6ème primaire

The terminal cycle of basic education includes:

7ème année
8ème année

Secondary education / humanities includes configurable pathways and options.

The official Ministry of Education currently describes general, technical and professional humanities.

IMPORTANT:

Do not hard-code every possible option or assume every school offers the same programs.

The application must be configurable.

Official terminology and rules can evolve.

Where a rule is uncertain or establishment-dependent:

MAKE IT CONFIGURABLE.

Never invent a legal rule.

"Institut"

- Other / configurable

B. ESTABLISHMENT FORM / NAME

Examples:

- École
- Complexe scolaire

- Groupe scolaire
- Other

C. EDUCATIONAL LEVELS

Examples:

- Maternelle
- Primaire
- 7ème–8ème
- Humanités générales
- Humanités techniques
- Humanités professionnelles
- Other configurable programs

These three concepts must never be conflated.

5. MULTI-SCHOOL ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Zeno must be multi-tenant.

One school must NEVER access another school's data.

Every important entity must belong to the appropriate school/tenant.

Users may belong to one or multiple schools if the architecture supports it.

Permissions must be evaluated server-side.

Never rely only on frontend hiding.

The existing access guard must be preserved and improved, not replaced unnecessarily.

The application must always resolve:

school
→ user
→ role
→ permissions

before rendering protected product data.
